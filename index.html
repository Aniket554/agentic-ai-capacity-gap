Same failure modes. Thinner safety net.

Qi et al. (2026) mapped how agentic AI fails across five stages — perceive, plan, act, reflect, learn. This tool walks that same map from a resource-constrained vantage point: what breaks, why it breaks worse here, and what a two-person security team can actually do about it.

Companion resource for the review article Trustworthy Agentic AI in the Global South (2026). Figures here are illustrative reconstructions of the article's conceptual framing, not published indices.

Walk the agent lifecycle

Select a stage to see where trust breaks down and what a lean team can do first. Bar length shows how much attention that stage draws in the risk literature — Act dominates, because it's where a mistake becomes irreversible.

The capacity gap behind the technology

The same agent framework ships to a fintech start-up in Nairobi and a bank in Frankfurt. What doesn't travel with it is everything around the technology. Compare two illustrative deployment profiles across five dimensions.
